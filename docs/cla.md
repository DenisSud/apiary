# Contributor copyright assignment

The project's position depends on a single copyright: the MIT license is
granted by one licensor, and any future relicensing or dual licensing is
only clean if the licensor owns (or holds equivalent rights to) the whole
work. Copyright on code arises automatically in its author, so every
outside contribution must be assigned before it is merged.

This applies to every PR from a repo account other than the maintainer's.
No merge without it.

## The agreement

By submitting a pull request, patch or other contribution to this
repository, and posting the assignment line below from the account that owns
that contribution, you agree to the following:

1. You assign to **Denis Sudakov** all right, title and interest in and to
   the copyright and every other exclusive economic right in the
   contribution, worldwide, for the full term of those rights, without
   additional consideration. The assignment takes effect when the
   contribution is incorporated into the repository and applies to every
   past and future contribution from your account covered by this section.
2. The assignment includes the right to relicense, sublicense, modify,
   combine and enforce those rights, and to hold or transfer them in any way
   the project requires — including under commercial terms.
3. You confirm that the contribution is your original work or work you are
   authorized to assign, that you have the legal power to make the
   assignment, and that, to your knowledge, it does not infringe any
   third-party right.
4. To the extent permitted by the law that governs your moral rights
   (attribution, integrity) in the contribution, you consent to any
   dealings with the contribution the project sees fit. Where moral rights
   are inalienable (for example, under Russian law), this clause applies to
   the extent actually permitted and no further.
5. In return, you receive a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, irrevocable
   license to use, modify and redistribute your own contributions in any
   form — the assignment never restricts your own code.

Contributions derived from third-party sources (a port, a translation of
MIT-licensed code, a snippet from elsewhere) can only be assigned as far as
your rights in them reach. State any such origin in the PR, so the
assignment's coverage is known before merge.

## How to sign

Post this line as a comment on the pull request, from the same GitHub
account that submits it:

    I accept the contributor copyright assignment in docs/cla.md.

That comment is the signed acceptance; it stays in the PR history as the
record. Reviewers check it before merge, alongside the test gate. If the
contribution volume ever justifies it, replace this manual check with a CLA
service (for example a cla-assistant.io GitHub App) bound to this document.
